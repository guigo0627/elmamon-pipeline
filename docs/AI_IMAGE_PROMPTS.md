## 🎨 LOGO - Facebook Profile (320x320px)

### Prompt para Gemini/DALL-E

```
Create a minimalist logo for a comedy channel called "ElMamón - Situaciones Cotidianas"

COMPOSITION:
- Square format 1:1 (320x320px)
- Two stick figure characters in hand-drawn style
- Left character: Female with 5 curved hair lines falling to the sides, white body, apologetic pose with hands together, small "o" mouth
- Right character: Male with 3 short hair lines on top, white body, arms crossed, straight line mouth, submissive expression
- Both characters: large cartoon eyes, irregular hand-drawn outlines, white skin and clothing, black shoes

BACKGROUND:
- Solid vibrant blue (#2E5EFF) or purple (#9D4EFF)
- Clean, modern, professional

TEXT:
- "ElMamón" in bold white letters at top
- "Situaciones Cotidianas" in smaller white text below title
- Drop shadow for text readability

STYLE:
- Minimalist
- Hand-drawn/sketch aesthetic
- Professional social media logo
- High contrast for small sizes
- Recognizable at thumbnail size

TECHNICAL:
- Clean lines
- Web-optimized
- Transparent or solid background
- SVG-ready design
```

### Versión Corta (para Gemini rápido)

```
Square minimalist logo, 1:1 ratio. Two white hand-drawn stick figures on vibrant blue background. Left: female with hair lines, hands together, "o" mouth. Right: male, arms crossed, straight mouth. Both with large cartoon eyes, irregular lines, black shoes. White bold text "ElMamón" at top, smaller "Situaciones Cotidianas" below. Professional, clean, recognizable at small size.
```

## 🖼️ BANNER - Facebook Cover (820x312px)

### Prompt para Gemini/DALL-E

```
Create a Facebook cover banner for a comedy channel "ElMamón - Situaciones Cotidianas"

COMPOSITION:
- Horizontal banner format 820x312px (approximately 2.63:1 ratio)
- Two hand-drawn stick figure characters positioned left and right
- Large central text area
- Balanced, professional layout

LEFT CHARACTER (Female):
- White stick figure with hand-drawn irregular outlines
- 5 curved hair lines falling from top to sides
- Large cartoon eyes looking forward
- Open "O" mouth (talking/apologetic)
- Hands together in apologetic gesture
- White body and clothing
- Black shoes
- Positioned at 10% from left edge

RIGHT CHARACTER (Male):
- White stick figure with hand-drawn irregular outlines
- 3 short hair lines on top of head
- Large cartoon eyes
- Straight line mouth (neutral/submissive)
- Arms crossed
- White body and clothing
- Black shoes
- Positioned at 75% from left edge

BACKGROUND:
- Vibrant gradient from blue (#2E5EFF) to purple (#9D4EFF)
- Top to bottom gradient
- Smooth, modern, eye-catching

CENTRAL TEXT:
- Main title "Situaciones Cotidianas" in center
- Large, bold, white font (64px equivalent)
- Drop shadow for depth
- Subtitle "Videos cómicos de la vida real 😂" in golden yellow (#FFD700)
- Call-to-action "¡SÍGUENOS! 👉" in orange box (#FF6B35) with white border
- All text centered

STYLE:
- Hand-drawn/sketch aesthetic for characters
- Modern, professional layout
- High energy, inviting
- Comedy/humor vibe
- Social media optimized

MOOD:
- Fun
- Relatable
- Inviting
- Professional yet casual
- Engaging

TECHNICAL:
- Sharp details
- Web-optimized
- High contrast text
- Readable at all sizes
```

### Versión Corta (para Gemini rápido)

```
Horizontal banner 820x312px. Blue-to-purple gradient background. Two white stick figures: left female with hair, apologetic pose; right male, arms crossed. Both hand-drawn style, large cartoon eyes, black shoes. Center: large white text "Situaciones Cotidianas", gold subtitle "Videos cómicos de la vida real 😂", orange button "¡SÍGUENOS! 👉". Professional comedy channel aesthetic.
```

## 📊 ESPECIFICACIONES TÉCNICAS

### Logo (Perfil de Facebook)
```
Formato: PNG con transparencia (o JPG con fondo sólido)
Tamaño: 320x320 píxeles
Ratio: 1:1 (cuadrado)
Peso: < 100 KB
Resolución: 72 DPI (web)
Espacio de color: RGB
```

### Banner (Portada de Facebook)
```
Formato: PNG o JPG
Tamaño: 820x312 píxeles  
Ratio: 2.63:1 (horizontal)
Peso: < 100 KB
Resolución: 72 DPI (web)
Espacio de color: RGB
Área segura: Centrar elementos importantes (evitar bordes)
```
